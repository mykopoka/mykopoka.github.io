Detailed Introduction

Kopoka HR SaaS for attendance, payroll, and integrated HR operations.

Kopoka is a cloud-based HR SaaS product that helps organizations record attendance from smartphone, process payroll, and manage HR data with one connected system.

Login to App View Guide

What you get

  • Attendance with selfie and location verification
  • Requests for leave, overtime, and attendance correction
  • Payroll and tax support, including PPh 21 processing
  • Eight integrated HRIS modules in web app

Access

Web, Android, iOS

SaaS

Operational Benefits

Value for staff, managers, and HR/finance

Staff

Clock in/out with smartphone, report visits, submit requests, and manage shift exchange directly from the app.

Manager / Superior

Monitor attendance and visit reports in real time, then approve requests quickly from smartphone.

HR / Finance

Collect attendance data automatically, process personnel administration, and calculate payroll with better consistency.

Organization
